# Vision

CommandMesh exists to give enterprises control over agentic AI systems.

As companies adopt AI agents, they lose visibility into:
- which models are being used
- where sensitive data is going
- what tools agents are calling
- how much inference is costing
- whether outputs are compliant and auditable

CommandMesh aims to become the control plane that sits between users, applications, agents, tools, and models.

The long-term goal is to make enterprise AI:
- governable
- observable
- secure
- cost-aware
- operationally reliable
