# Problem Statement

Enterprises are rapidly adopting AI, but most lack a professional control layer for model and agent usage.

This creates several problems:
- employees use unapproved AI tools
- prompts and outputs are not audited
- sensitive data may be exposed to external models
- AI costs become unpredictable
- agents may call tools without sufficient guardrails
- security and compliance teams have limited visibility

Existing infrastructure was designed for traditional software systems, not autonomous or semi-autonomous AI workloads.

CommandMesh is being designed to solve this by acting as a centralized agentic AI governance and routing layer.
